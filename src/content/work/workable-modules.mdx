### People and teams

Certification tracking for the whole team, synced from Microsoft Learn through an Azure Function that sits next to the database. 30/60/90-day onboarding plans with check-ins and shared notes. KPI and KRA reviews, and a redesign of performance reviews that is underway.

### Reaching people

A marketing campaigns module in the CRM. Contacts go straight into LinkedIn and Meta ad audiences through their APIs. Large campaigns send from the server in batches, so they finish even if the page is closed. Sales booking links that check only the people who need to be there, with a bot that joins each booked meeting to capture the transcript.

### AI sales calls

Calls spread across a first-call and last-call window in Sydney time. A rule per script for when a phone's call-screening assistant answers. Calls that dropped the moment they connected traced to an unfilled placeholder in the opening line, fixed with a check that catches it next time.

</Section>

<Section label="§ 2 Keeping it fast">

Unfiltered live updates on four busy pages were exhausting the database's connections; removing them stopped the recurring incidents. Contact filters moved to the server so they search every page of results. A six-hourly sync became an on-demand, cached function.

</Section>
