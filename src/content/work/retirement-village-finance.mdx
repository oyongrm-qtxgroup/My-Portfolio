### A fee engine that matches the finance team's spreadsheet

The deferred management fee, with daily and capped accrual, spread across the actual days in each month so April and May no longer show identical figures. Annual CPI-plus-margin increases from 1 July, behind an approval step with a full audit trail. Validated line by line against the finance team's own spreadsheet, and kept as an automated test.

### Billing that posts real accounting

Finalising a billing run writes the journal entries too, not just what residents owe, including charges that cross entities. Café purchases come in from the point of sale. Statement lines say what a resident is paying for: the charge, the rate and the period.

### Money in, months closed

The month's direct-debit file for the bank. Payment due dates roll forward to the next business day, with state public holidays kept in the system. Once Finance locks a month for an entity, nothing can be posted into it.

</Section>
