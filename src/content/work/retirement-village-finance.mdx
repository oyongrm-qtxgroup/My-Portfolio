<Section label="§ 1 The problem" ruled>

A retirement-village operator bills residents under contracts that can run for decades: a management fee that builds up daily and is settled when they leave, yearly increases tied to inflation, café tabs and direct debits. The numbers have to match the finance team's own records exactly, because residents and auditors will check them.

### A fee engine the finance team can check

The management fee and its yearly CPI increases are calculated to match the finance team's own spreadsheet to the cent, and that spreadsheet's example now runs as a test on every change.

### Billing that keeps the books straight

A billing run writes the accounting entries as well as what each resident owes, produces statements that say what each charge is for, and creates the month's direct-debit file for the bank. Once Finance closes a month, nothing can be posted into it.

### Built in the room with Finance

I ran the walkthroughs with the finance team, covering the whole resident journey from moving in to leaving and being refunded, and rebuilt the exit process to their lead's specification.
