<Section label="The challenge" ruled>

A dental receptionist can’t answer the phone while checking a patient in, and a missed call is often a lost booking. Practices needed something that answers every call, books into the diary they already use, and sounds like part of their team rather than a phone menu.

</Section>

<Section label="The approach" ruled>

<FigRef n={1} />

### Sound like the practice’s own receptionist

The agent follows a licensed library of dental-practice scripts. Each practice gets one voice that stays the same from call to call, turn-taking that stops the agent talking over people, and the real date and time on every call, so “next Tuesday” means the right day.

<FigRef n={2} />

### One script, three booking systems

<Noted notes={['3 practice systems, 1 script', '4 tools the agent may call']}>

CareStack, Core Practice and Dentally sit behind a single receptionist script and the same four tools. The number a caller rings from finds their record, and the agent confirms their name before it reads out, moves or cancels anything, which also covers patients on file under a different spelling.

</Noted>

<FigRef n={3} />

### How every change is evaluated

Practices ask for script changes in plain English. Before a change goes live, it runs through four or five scripted scenarios and is compared with past calls; an LLM judge scores each transcript against the expected outcome, and an admin reads the results before approving it. Every version can be restored.

### Data handling

<Noted notes={['Callers are told it’s an AI at the start']}>

Call transcripts and logs are stored in Supabase; recordings stay with ElevenLabs and Twilio under their own retention settings. Callers are told at the start that they are speaking to an AI assistant and that the call may be recorded.

</Noted>

### What I built

I took over a working prototype in April 2026 and lead its development with one or two other developers. Since then I’ve built the three practice-system integrations, the review workflow, failure alerts with a 15-minute health check, and billing.

</Section>

Jayne Bandy AI is live, answering and booking calls in practices’ own systems. A failure at an AI provider now raises an alert within minutes instead of being noticed by a practice. Usage figures belong to QTX and aren’t published.
