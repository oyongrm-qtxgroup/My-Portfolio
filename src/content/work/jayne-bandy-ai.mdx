<Section label="§ 1 The problem" ruled>

A dental receptionist can't answer the phone while they're checking a patient in, and a missed call is often a lost booking. Jayne Bandy AI answers instead: it talks to the caller, finds a free slot in the practice's own booking system and books it, or takes a callback when it can't. I took over as lead engineer in April 2026.

</Section>

### Made it sound like the practice's receptionist

One voice per practice that stays the same from call to call, turn-taking that stops it talking over people, and the real date and time on every call so "next Tuesday" means the right day.

### One script, three booking systems

CareStack, Core Practice and Dentally sit behind a single receptionist script. Callers are recognised by the number they ring from, even when the name on file is spelt differently, so the agent finds their appointment instead of asking twice.

### Changes get tested before a caller hears them

Practices ask for script changes in plain English. Before an admin approves one, it runs against standard scenarios and replays of real calls, and every version can be restored. Practices can't see or copy the prompt behind their receptionist.
