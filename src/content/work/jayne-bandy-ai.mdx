<Section label="§ 1 The challenge" ruled>

A dental receptionist can't answer the phone while checking a patient in, and a missed call is often a lost booking. Practices needed something that answers every call, books into the diary they already use, and sounds like part of their team rather than a phone menu.

</Section>

<Section
  label="§ 2 The approach"
  ruled
  notes={['Prospects hear their own accent: each country has its own demo number.']}
>

### Sound like the practice's own receptionist

Each practice gets one voice that stays the same from call to call, turn-taking that stops the agent talking over people, and the real date and time on every call, so "next Tuesday" means the right day.

### One script, three booking systems

CareStack, Core Practice and Dentally sit behind a single receptionist script. Callers are recognised by the number they ring from, even when the name on file is spelt differently, so the agent finds their appointment instead of asking twice.

### Test every change before a caller hears it

Practices ask for script changes in plain English. Before an admin approves one, it runs against standard scenarios and replays of real calls, and every version can be restored. Practices can't see or copy the prompt behind their receptionist.

</Section>

<Section label="§ 3 The result" ruled>

Jayne Bandy AI is live, answering and booking calls in practices' own systems. A health check runs every 15 minutes and failures raise an alert, so a problem with an AI provider is caught in minutes rather than noticed by a practice. New practices start on an automatic trial and can move to a premium tier with a custom voice, with subscriptions kept in step with Stripe.

</Section>
