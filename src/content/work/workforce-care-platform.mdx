<Section label="§ 1 The problem" ruled>

A disability-support provider pays its support workers under the SCHADS award and bills each participant's NDIS funding. Every shift has to turn into the right pay, the right invoice against the right funding, and the right entry in the accounts, and a mistake at any step lands on a worker's payslip or a participant's plan.

### Pay that follows the award

Award pay rates and penalties apply to every shift, sleepovers are paid and billed as their own case, and support workers clock on from their phone. A timesheet can't be approved while billed hours and worked hours disagree.

### Money that only moves when a person says so

Invoices are batched by funding type and exported to MYOB with payroll. Payment files no longer close invoices on their own: the system suggests which invoice each line belongs to, and Finance approves it.

### Testing with the people who use it

I worked through the client's test rounds directly, fixing, releasing and checking each item on the live site until the defect register was empty.
