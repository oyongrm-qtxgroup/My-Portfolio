### Rostering and pay that follow the award

SCHADS award pay rates and penalties applied to every shift, sleepover shifts handled as their own case for pay and billing, and roster rules the system enforces instead of trusting. Support workers clock on from their phone; admins can enter a missed clock-in with a reason.

### Invoicing a finance team can trust

Invoices are batched and reviewed by funding type, with an NDIS claim file at the end. Approving a timesheet is blocked when billed hours don't match hours worked. Payment files no longer close invoices on their own: the system suggests a match and Finance approves each line.

### Accounting, recruitment and compliance

Payroll and sales export to MYOB. Candidate intake where a résumé upload fills in the details. A daily job that retires expired credentials, warns managers ahead of time and recalculates who can be rostered.
