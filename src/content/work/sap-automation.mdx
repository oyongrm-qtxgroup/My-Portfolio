<Section label="§ 1 The challenge" ruled>

Two jobs at Ortigas Land took people's time without needing their judgement. Staff keyed invoices, reversals and contract locks into SAP by hand, and HR spent more than 100 hours a month processing government remittances. Both followed fixed rules, which made them good candidates for a bot.

</Section>

<Section label="§ 2 The approach" ruled>

### Use SAP the way staff do

The SAP bot drives the same desktop client staff use and follows the same steps a person would, so SAP itself didn't have to change to be automated.

### Read the documents instead of retyping them

The remittance bot uses AI document extraction to read the figures from HR's source documents, and Power Automate carries the remittance through from there.

### Lead the team that builds them

I led a four-person development team and introduced structured code reviews and secure development practices alongside the automation work.

</Section>

<Section label="§ 3 The result" ruled>

Manual SAP processing time fell by 70–80%, and more than 100 hours a month of HR work moved from people to the remittance bot.

</Section>
