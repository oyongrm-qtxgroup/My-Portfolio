<Section label="The challenge" ruled>

A disability-support provider ran rostering, timesheets and invoicing across spreadsheets. Every shift has to become the right pay under the SCHADS award, the right invoice against the participant’s National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) funding and the right entry in MYOB, and a mistake in any of them costs a worker or the provider money.

</Section>

<Section label="The approach" ruled>

<FigRef n={1} />

### Award rules as configuration

<Noted notes={['Award rules are set by admins, not written into code']}>

SCHADS pay rates and penalty rules are configured by admins in the app rather than written into code, so a rate change doesn’t wait for a developer. Each approved shift is priced from those rules for pay, and invoiced against the participant’s funding type.

</Noted>

### Access that follows the organisation

<Noted notes={['Access is checked in the database']}>

Each person’s reach comes from their role (worker, coordinator, manager or Finance) and the teams, sites and programs they’re assigned to. Row-level security in the database checks those links, so the rule holds whichever screen or API asks.

</Noted>

<FigRef n={2} />

### Reconciliation Finance approves

<Noted notes={['Finance approves line by line']}>

Finance uploads the bank file, the platform suggests which invoice each line belongs to, and Finance approves one line at a time. A line with no clear match stays open instead of being guessed.

</Noted>

### Built to fail safely

A daily job flags workers whose credentials have expired, so they can’t be rostered, and warns managers before the next ones lapse. Finance signs off sample pay runs before each release.

### What I built

I lead Ziaris and built most of it with one or two other developers: rostering, award-based pay, timesheets, invoicing by funding type, the MYOB exports, reconciliation, row-level security, and a staff assistant that answers questions with Azure OpenAI.

</Section>

<Section label="The result" ruled>

Ziaris is live. Rostering, timesheets and invoicing moved from spreadsheets into one system; each shift’s pay comes from the configured award rules instead of being worked out by hand; payroll and invoices reach MYOB without re-keying; and Finance approves suggested payment matches instead of matching them by hand.

</Section>
