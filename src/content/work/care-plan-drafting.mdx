### Evidence first

A versioned clinical record for each client: a summary of every document and key facts by domain, each linked to its page. Every statement in a draft links to its document page, quoted passage or assessment answer. Contradictions, gaps and outdated, undated or unreadable evidence become review items that the reviewer acknowledges; nothing is resolved silently.

### Drafting that survives a closed tab

Drafts run on the server, so a two-minute draft keeps going if the user reloads or closes the tab, and its progress shows from any tab. Each draft is marked as AI-assisted and names the model that wrote it.

### Access and approvals

Microsoft sign-in creates the staff record, and every permission is checked on the server. Only clinical reviewers can approve a plan; returning, resubmitting, approving and withdrawing are all recorded, and approving your own plan is blocked by default.
