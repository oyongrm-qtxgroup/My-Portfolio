### Contracts that get signed

A Contract Manager agent built to the product lead's requirements, and SEED Sign, a standalone e-signature flow with routing and notifications that sits behind it. Tested end to end together before release.

### Chat that knows what to do next

Answers come back as cards you can act on, and the starter prompts are generated from the user's own data and available actions instead of being the same for everyone. Off-topic questions are refused and repeat answers cached, which cut the cost of the chat.

### Boards, people and roles

Boardroom set-up no longer needs a tenant ID. Users are invited from the company directory, and roles are delegated by picking people, not typing names. Tenant isolation went in before the first client deployment. Document generation uses Azure Document Intelligence, configured from the settings page.
