---
title: What "next Tuesday" means
date: 2026-08-04
work: jayne-bandy-ai
summary: The receptionist didn't know what day it was, so it guessed, and a guessed date in a booking is a wrong appointment.
---

Callers say "tomorrow" and "next Tuesday". The receptionist didn't know what today was, so it worked it out from context, and a guessed date in a booking conversation is a wrong appointment.

Every call now starts with the real date, time and the practice's own time zone. The same change stopped it promising things it couldn't do: a practice with no booking system connected gets a natural conversation instead of a confirmation it can't keep.

Language models are confident about things nobody told them. Most of the reliability work was deciding what the model must be told on every call, rather than hoping it would infer it.
