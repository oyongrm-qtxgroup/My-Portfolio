---
title: Saying no is cheaper
date: 2026-02-27
summary: The cheapest improvement to an AI chat was teaching it to refuse questions it had no business answering.
---

SEED's agents answer questions about an organisation's contracts and compliance documents. People also asked them everything else.

Each off-topic answer cost money and, worse, sounded authoritative about things the system knew nothing about. Teaching the chat to decline questions outside its documents, and caching answers to questions asked again and again, cut the cost of running it without making it less useful.

A focused assistant that says "that's not something I can help with" earns more trust than one that will happily talk about anything.
