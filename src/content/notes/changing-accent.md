---
title: The receptionist kept changing accent
date: 2026-09-02
work: jayne-bandy-ai
summary: Every call worked, but not as the same person, because the voice engine never held the accent between calls.
---

A practice could hear an Australian receptionist at nine and an American one at ten. Nothing in their settings had changed, and nothing was technically broken: every call connected and every booking worked.

The complaint was simple: "it sounds different every time". The cause sat underneath our settings. The voice engine didn't hold the accent from one call to the next, so the choice in our admin screen was being quietly ignored. I removed the cause rather than adding another setting on top of it.

Consistency is a feature. A receptionist that sounds like a different person each time doesn't sound like the practice's receptionist.
