---
title: Nothing happened, and nobody was told
date: 2026-09-16
work: workforce-care-platform
summary: The worst defects in a disability-care platform were the silent ones, so money now moves only when a person approves it.
---

Three bugs from the same platform, all the same shape. A bulk email screen said "sent" when nothing went out. Uploading a payment file quietly marked invoices as paid. A roster's staff list quietly left out anyone whose recorded program didn't match.

None of them threw an error. Each looked like success to the person using it, which is worse than a crash, because nobody goes looking.

Emails now go through the provider with a receipt for every message. Payment files no longer close anything on their own: the system suggests which invoice each line belongs to, and Finance approves one line at a time. I now treat "nothing happened and nobody was told" as its own kind of bug.
