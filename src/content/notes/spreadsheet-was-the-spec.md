---
title: The spreadsheet was the spec
date: 2026-08-13
work: retirement-village-finance
summary: The finance team's own workbook was the real specification, so I matched it to the cent and made it a test.
---

For a retirement-village operator, the deferred management fee is the number everything else hangs off: what a resident owes each month, and what they get back when they leave.

The finance team had a spreadsheet they had trusted for years. Instead of arguing with it, we checked the system against it on screen, line by line, year by year, including how each year's fee spreads across the actual days in each month. Once it matched to the cent, their example became an automated test that runs on every change.

From then on, a question about a number had an answer that either passed or didn't. Next time I'll ask for that spreadsheet in the first week.
