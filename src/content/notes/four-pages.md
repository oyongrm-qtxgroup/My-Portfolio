---
title: Four pages took the database down
date: 2026-04-29
summary: A few busy screens listening for every change in the database exhausted its connections, so I now price a screen before it ships.
---

Workable is QTX's own operating platform, and building for colleagues who sit next to you is fast. That speed hides costs.

Four busy pages, the backlog, a personal work board and two inboxes, each subscribed to every live change in their tables, unfiltered. That was enough to exhaust the database's connections and cause the same incident again and again.

Removing those unfiltered subscriptions stopped the incidents. Since then I check what a new screen costs the database before it ships, not after the first outage.
