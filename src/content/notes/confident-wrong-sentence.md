title: A confident wrong sentence
date: 2026-10-21
work: care-plan-drafting
summary: In a care plan, a fluent sentence nobody can check is worse than no sentence, so every statement shows its source.
---

The tool drafts aged-care plans from a client's records. The easy version writes fluent paragraphs. The useful version lets a clinician check every line quickly.

So every statement in a draft links to the page, quoted passage or assessment answer it came from. Where the records disagree, or are outdated, undated or unreadable, the draft doesn't pick a side: it raises a review item the clinician has to acknowledge. Each draft is marked as AI-assisted and names the model that wrote it.

The measure of an AI draft isn't how good it reads. It's how quickly a professional can tell whether to trust it.
